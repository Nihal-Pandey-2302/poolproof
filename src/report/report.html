Each scenario runs against plain Postgres, then through the pooler. A cell fails when the pooler's result differs from Postgres. Silent failures are the dangerous kind: the application gets a wrong result and no error. Some failures are documented limitations of the pooler: they are expected, and still break apps that rely on the feature. Click a cell for the full trace.
